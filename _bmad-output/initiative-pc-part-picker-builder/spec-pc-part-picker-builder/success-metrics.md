# Success metrics (v1)

Cross-linked to capabilities and `functional-requirements.md` FRs.

## Primary

- **SM-1: Compatible build completion rate** — Among Builds started (context set + ≥1 Slot filled), % reaching saved Compatible Build within one session. **Target ≥45% within 90 days of launch.** (FR-8, FR-9, FR-11, FR-13, FR-19)
- **SM-2: Budget-successful saves** — Among saved Compatible Builds, % at or under budget at save time. **Target ≥70%.** (FR-1, FR-10, FR-19)

## Secondary

- **SM-3: Return edit rate** — % of saved Builds edited at least once within 14 days. **Target ≥30%.** (FR-20, FR-26, FR-27)
- **SM-4: Share adoption** — % of saved Compatible Builds generating a share link within 30 days. **Target ≥20%.** (FR-22, FR-23)
- **SM-5: Catalog freshness SLA** — See `catalog-freshness.md`. **Target ≥95% rolling 7-day** for **active-catalog** (top-parts) Parts with Amazon mappings.

## Counter-metrics (do not optimize)

- **SM-C1:** Invalid Builds forced to save/share — **0**.
- **SM-C2:** Parts beyond staleness threshold shown without badge — **0**.
